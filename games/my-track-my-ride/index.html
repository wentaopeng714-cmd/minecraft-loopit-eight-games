Hello, Oakvale!

A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Make tracks.

  1. Draw your railway. Follow the dotted guide. Drag or tap the glowing tiles. Rails snap into place, and gaps connect automatically.
  2. Reach the station. Connect the green destination, then choose Ride Your Track.
  3. Watch the Creepers. Hold the green button to move. Release to stop at a red crossing, then hold again when it turns green. You have three hearts; a bump keeps your route and progress.

Scoring: arrive +500, each emerald +100, each heart left on arrival +100. Earn arrival, full-cargo and clean-ride stamps. Delivered routes are saved in your routebook.
