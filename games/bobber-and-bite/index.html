Bobber & Bite

  1. Tap CAST. A dipping bobber and the BITE cue tell you when to start reeling.
  2. Hold REEL to bring the fish closer. Release before the tension needle reaches red.
  3. Catch 5 fish to fill the basket. New trips add stronger fish and new catches.
