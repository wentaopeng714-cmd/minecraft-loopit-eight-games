THE SHORT VERSION

A little explosive.

  1. Drag on the arena to aim. The dotted arc and landing ring show where your Creeper will go. Release to throw.
  2. Move away from red circles. Use the left stick, WASD or arrow keys. A flashing Creeper is about to explode.
  3. Break cover. Beat the rival. Aim behind their wall or blast it apart. Empty the red health bar to win. Choose NEXT RIVAL to keep your streak. Each new duel restores your health and cover.

Tap TOSS for a quick throw. Score: 100 per HP of actual damage, 25 per broken block, 300 for a win, and 50 per heart left when you win.
